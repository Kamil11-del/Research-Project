All experiments follow a consistent 5-fold cross-validation protocol. Results reported per split correspond to the best-epoch performance for each metric group.

P74: 9. Analysis & Discussion
P75: 9.1  TAS Performance
P76: V2 (Post-Encoding Mask Tokens) achieves the highest TAS average of 90.11, marginally exceeding V1 (89.63), V3 (89.77), and V5 (89.18). The common thread among top TAS performers (V1ÔÇôV3, V5) is that they all operate with mask tokens at or before the encoding stage, preserving the encoder's ability to reason over the full temporal context. V4 (80.14) and V6 (76.54) show considerably weaker TAS performance, suggesting their architectural changes are detrimental to segmentation quality.
P78: 9.2  LTA Performance
P79: V3 achieves the best LTA average of 35.93, followed by V1 (33.01) and V4 (31.89). The dedicated per-task mask tokens introduced in V3 yield consistent LTA improvements across all observation-prediction horizons. V2 drops sharply to 25.03 in LTA, confirming that post-encoding masking disrupts the anticipation pathway: the encoder must have access to mask tokens early to reason about unobserved future actions. V5 (25.71) and V6 (25.93) also underperform on LTA.
P81: 9.3  Key Findings
P83: V3 (separate TAS/LTA mask tokens, pre-encoding) achieves the best overall balance, with top LTA (35.93) and competitive TAS (89.77).
P84: V2 posts the best TAS (90.11) but at a significant LTA cost (25.03, ÔêÆ8.0 vs V1), confirming post-encoding masking disrupts anticipation.
P85: V1 remains a strong baseline across both tasks (TAS 89.63, LTA 33.01), with the advantage of architectural simplicity.
P86: V4 shows moderate LTA (31.89) but weak TAS (80.14), suggesting the design favors anticipation at the expense of segmentation.
P87: V5 and V6 consistently underperform on LTA (25.71 and 25.93), indicating their design choices are suboptimal for anticipation tasks.
P88: The largest single performance gap across all comparisons is V3 vs V2 on LTA (+10.90), highlighting how critically the position of mask token insertion impacts anticipation quality.
